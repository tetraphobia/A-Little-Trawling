# 1. Title Page
## 1.1 Game Name
A Little Trawling
# 2. Game Overview
## 2.1 Game Concept
A relaxing, single-player (and possibly cooperative)/ fishing game. Sail around a scenic island in a small trawling ship. Sell fish to upgrade your ship to catch larger, more valuable fish and hunt for treasures.
## 2.2 Genre
Cozy, exploration.
## 2.3 Target Audience
Casual players.
## 2.4 Game Flow Summary
Player starts on an island and moves around the environment in 3D. Player boards a ship and can sail the ship around by entering the driver seat. Player navigates to schools of fish to fish them.
## 2.5 Look and Feel
A cartoony, retro feel. PS1-era graphics. Character design is reminescent of games like Animal Crossing. 
# 3. Gameplay and Mechanics
## 3.1 Gameplay
### 3.1.1 Game Progression
Player posesses a simple fishing rod and a shanty boat. Upgrades to the boat may include a trawling net for mass-fish catching, upgrades to boat storage, speed, a crane to salvage wrecks. The player's rod may also be upgraded to catch fish more easily.
### 3.1.2 Mission Structure
N/A
### 3.1.3 Puzzle Structure
Possibly some puzzles involving catching certain kinds of fish and offering them on an altar to unlock boat parts.
### 3.1.4 Objectives
Catch fish, upgrade gear to catch more valuable fish, catch more valuable fish.
### 3.1.5 Play Flow
## 3.2 Mechanics
### 3.2.1 Physics
Minimal physics, but players will need to move along with the boat, which may introduce physics complications.
### 3.2.2 Movement In-Game
Horizontal movement across a plane. No jumping.
### 3.2.3 Objects
Storage bins in the boat, driver seat.
### 3.2.4 Actions
Fishing in schools of fish. Interacting to store fish in barrels. Interacting with fishmongers.
### 3.2.5 Combat
N/A
### 3.2.6 Economy
Incremental economy. Higher tier upgrades cost significantly more than lower tier upgrades. More valuabe fish must be caught to cover the cost in a reasonable amount of time.
## 3.3 Game Options
None for the prototype.

Rebindable keys and accessibility features (motion sickness, vertigo, color blindness)
## 3.4 Replaying and Saving
Game saves automatically when purchasing upgrades and when ending the day.
## 3.5 Cheats and Easter Eggs
Maybe
# 4. Story, Setting and Character
## 4.1 Story and Narrative
The player character inherits their grandfather's fishing trawler. You go out to catch fish to feed the local populace and find treasures to help the town prosper.
## 4.2 Game World
A vast ocean world filled with various islands. Think Wind Waker. An "old world" that flooded long ago that contains remnants of a forgotten civilization.
### 4.2.1 General Look and Feel of World
Depends on the area. Default look would be a bright and colorful environment. Colors will get warmer and dimmer as sunset approaches.
### 4.2.2 Areas
One small island area for the demo along with surrounding ocean, possibly more later.
## 4.3 Characters
Player character is a cat creature named Filip.
